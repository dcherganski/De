Project privacy notice · Draft

Privacy

This notice is a plain-language project draft. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer and will be replaced by a formal privacy policy before KATE collects any personal data.

This website

  • The site is a set of static pages. It has no user accounts, no sign-up and no contact form.
  • It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • It loads no fonts, scripts or images from third parties.
  • The hosting provider that serves these pages may keep standard technical server logs (for example IP address and time of the request) for security and operation, under its own policy.

If you write to us

The "Contact" links open your own email app. If you send an email, the founder receives your address and whatever you write, and uses it only to reply to you. You can ask for that correspondence to be deleted at any time. Please don't send passwords, ID numbers or bank details.

The future KATE product

KATE is not yet available to the public and stores no user data today. The planned approach is:

  • Each user has a separate, private memory that is not shared with other users.
  • Users can see what KATE remembers about them.
  • Deleting your personal memory also permanently deletes the linked profile. The app will show a clear warning and ask for explicit confirmation before doing it.

This product rule does not replace anyone's legal rights. Before launch, the data handling will go through legal and technical review, and KATE will respect the rights users have under applicable law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — such as access, correction, deletion and data portability. Some information may have to be kept for a limited time where the law requires it; the formal policy will say what and for how long.
